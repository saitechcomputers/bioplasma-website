Delivery science

Absorption, engineered before it reaches you.

Conventional salts often suffer from poor dissolution or gastric irritation. We pair clinically proven actives with specialized delivery vehicles across our portfolio.

A

Nano-Droplet Oral D3

AADIRISE® D3 nano-emulsifies 60,000 IU Cholecalciferol for uniform aqueous dispersion in a 5 ml sugar-free shot.

B

Bioipc® encapsulates iron inside a protective bilayer alongside 4th-gen Quatrefolic® — eliminating metallic taste and constipation.

C

AdenoCal™-K2 utilizes sustainable red marine algae calcium (~86.5% bioavailability) with Vitamin D3 & K2-7.

D

PANTHETA™ DSR & RABTHETA™ DSR split Domperidone into 10 mg IR + 20 mg SR with Enteric-Coated PPIs.
