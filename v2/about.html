Next-gen science, timeless care.

Headquartered in Gurugram NCR, Bioplasma Biologicals Private Limited (LV Bioplasma) is dedicated to delivering patient-friendly, evidence-backed pharmaceutical and nutraceutical formulations.

LV Bioplasma emphasises clinical bioavailability in everything it formulates — from active ingredient sourcing and delivery matrices to palatable patient compliance.

Across everyday clinical practice, even proven molecules fall short when poor intestinal solubility, gastric acid degradation, or unpleasant taste disrupt patient adherence.

At Bioplasma Biologicals Private Limited, we solve that gap with thoughtful pharmaceutical engineering — nano-droplet Vitamin D3 shots, phospholipid-encapsulated Liposomal Iron, porous Irish marine-algae calcium, resilient probiotic spores, and split-release Tablet-in-Capsule gastro therapies.

Every product in our range is manufactured in WHO-GMP certified facilities and backed by transparent composition monographs for clinicians, distributors, and families alike.
